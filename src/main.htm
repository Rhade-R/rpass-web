Keys are hidden while you type. Peek shows them for 10 s. A press while they are visible types nothing, reshuffles the keys and adds 10 s. Protects against keystroke loggers and extensions reading the page, not against screen-recording malware.

v1 (legacy): 50 printable ASCII characters, may include spaces.

Safer: save a copy of this page, check its SHA-256 against the release, and open it from your disk, ideally in a private window. Extensions usually cannot access local files or private windows unless you allow it.
